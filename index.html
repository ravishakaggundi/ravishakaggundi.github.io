US visa application outcome prediction on 25,480 OFLC records. Compared 6 ensemble classifiers across 3 sampling strategies (original, SMOTE, undersampling) to maximize recall on certifiable candidates.

Best model: XGBoost + SMOTE — F1 0.81, Recall 0.85

Predicted 6-month customer churn for 10,000 bank customers (20.4% churn rate). Built 6 ANN variants comparing SGD vs Adam optimizers, dropout regularization, and SMOTE oversampling.
